Dungeon Scores publication and privacy summary
==============================================
Applies to DungeonScores 0.5.37 and the optional DungeonScoresRecords
Companion 0.5.15. This notice describes the bundled client's behavior and the
public-record data it exchanges; server-side retention and Discord delivery
are controlled by the community service.

LOCAL SCORING AND EXPLICIT PUBLICATION

Dungeon Scores works locally without the optional records companion. Installing
the companion does not automatically submit runs. A player must select a
finalized run, assign an exact registered team roster, and press "Send selected
score" to add a publication request to the SavedVariables queue. A completed
run can be submitted to more than one publication destination. Guild records
and guild events require their respective route selection. Race to the Bottom
requires its separate opt-in selection; eligibility alone does not enter a run.
An abandoned run can be queued only for Race to the Bottom, and only after the
addon's minimum credible dungeon-progress check succeeds.

The companion reads the local DungeonScores.lua SavedVariables file to locate
publicationQueue requests. It submits only their payloads; it does not upload
other run history, unselected personal bests, character names, chat, combat
logs, Encounter.log, BETA diagnostics, or unrelated settings. It never edits
DungeonScores.lua. A successful queue request does not guarantee placement in
any leaderboard.

INFORMATION SENT WITH AN EXPLICIT REQUEST

The queued payload contains:
- The public team name, the exact ESO @accountname roster, and the submitting
  ESO @accountname.
- PC megaserver, dungeon/zone ID and name, run ID, addon/schema versions,
  run outcome (completed or abandoned), and completion/finalization timestamps.
- Veteran and competitive-integrity indicators, credited boss count and any
  minimum credible-progress threshold, elapsed time, final party size and
  average party size, deaths, HM and trifecta results.
- Score components and final signed standard score, including vitality,
  tempo, and party-size adjustments. Only an explicit Race to the Bottom
  request adds the low-score result and applicable abandonment, abandoned-HM,
  NPC-companion, and pitstop deductions.
- An explicit Race to the Bottom requested/not-requested flag and eligibility
  flag. For a completed run, optional public guild/event identifiers, the
  permanent-guild-record request flag, and route-specific ESO membership-check
  booleans are included when applicable.

The player's complete ESO guild list is not uploaded. Route membership flags
are not a substitute for a teammate's consent to publication.

PUBLIC RECORDS AND DISCORD

The service processes and may retain accepted submissions and resulting
competitive standings across PC NA and PC EU in the relevant dungeon and
party-size divisions. This bundled client does not establish a
server-side retention period for displaced or rejected submissions.
Ordinary World Records rank higher scores by dungeon and party-size division
(with overall record views also supported). Guild records are scoped to their
configured guild, dungeon, and division. Race to the Bottom ranks lower scores
across all eligible dungeons in independent solo, duo, trio, and quartet
divisions; it can include completed runs and qualifying abandoned runs. Events
use their configured eligibility and placement rules.

Public record snapshots and Discord announcements may display team name and
ESO @accountname roster, score and placement, dungeon, megaserver, party size,
elapsed time, deaths, supported HM/trifecta results, addon version,
submission timestamps, and (for Race to the Bottom) completion/abandonment
status and applicable deductions. World Record and Race to the Bottom
standings support ordinal top-ten placements. Guild publication depth is
configured per guild (one through ten); event placements follow event rules.
Standings changes may produce refreshed public Discord messages in the
configured destination channels. The server, not the companion, decides
eligibility, rankings, and announcement delivery.

The companion also downloads public World Record, party-size, top-standing,
Race to the Bottom, guild, event, and opt-in guild-directory information. It
writes a sanitized data snapshot to DungeonScoresRecords/Records.lua for the
in-game browser. This file can contain other players' public team names and
ESO @accountnames, as well as configured guild recruitment/contact details.
The companion does not receive Discord bot credentials or private channel
configuration.

TEAM CONSENT AND VERIFIABILITY

Submit a team roster only after every listed teammate has agreed to public
identification by ESO @accountname. Public submissions and Discord messages
may remain visible even after a local score or installation is deleted.
Community-submitted results are not cryptographic proof of an in-game clear.

OPTIONAL BACKGROUND COMPANION AND LOCAL STORAGE

The companion is a separately installed .NET program. Its installer copies a
watcher into the current user's local application-data directory, starts it,
and creates a per-user login/startup entry. The watcher detects ESO activity,
checks the SavedVariables file for explicit requests, and refreshes public
records when ESO starts, when addon data is saved, after ESO exits, and about
every five minutes while ESO is running. An additional sync can occur if
SavedVariables changes while ESO is closed; otherwise there is no periodic
network activity while ESO is closed. Public-record refreshes can occur with
no new score to upload. Uploads use HTTPS POST /v1/runs and public-record
refreshes use HTTPS GET /v1/records at
https://dungeon-scores-api.sagofett.workers.dev. As with other web services,
the service can see connection metadata such as the requesting IP address
and request time. Service logging/retention is not established by this ZIP.

The optional control window opens in the player's default browser. While the
window is open, the companion listens only on a temporary local 127.0.0.1
address with an unguessable per-window path token. It does not expose the
controls on the network. Opening the page can start an already-enabled
watcher after an upgrade; that watcher follows its normal sync schedule.
Start/Stop manage the watcher;
the separate login switch manages the per-user startup entry. A manual Sync
uses the same explicit publication queue and public-record refresh as the
background watcher. Pressing Close controls closes the local listener; it
also closes after two minutes without browser activity.

Local application data includes the installed watcher/configuration, a
submitted-request-key ledger (submitted.json), a bounded delivery-status
ledger (delivery-status.json), watcher and latest-sync status, and a private
installation credential (credential.json). The credential is generated at the
first attempted queued upload unless the player supplied one explicitly; it
authenticates uploads and is not embedded in Records.lua or Delivery.lua.
The companion writes up to 100 recent local delivery outcomes to Delivery.lua
for the in-game browser. The ledgers and Delivery.lua use queue keys that can
contain team names and ESO @accountnames; keep them private. Delivery receipts
report server acceptance or local retry state, not leaderboard placement or
Discord delivery. Do not share the credential.
The installed startup watcher can be stopped and its login entry removed with
the matching Companion/Advanced/Disable-Startup launcher. Disabling or deleting
local files does not automatically remove published records or Discord posts.

CORRECTION OR DELETION REQUESTS

For a request to correct or remove published information, contact the Dungeon
Scores ESOUI support thread or the community support channel, identifying the
relevant team/record. Published Discord announcements may require separate
moderation or removal. This package does not specify a guaranteed server
retention period or an automated erasure schedule; ask the service operator
for current retention and deletion handling.
