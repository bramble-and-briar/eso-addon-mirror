Extract this GridBags folder into your ESO live/AddOns folder, then enable it and reload the UI.
Disable GridList, GridPadInventory and BankManagerRevived for initial testing.
No external libraries are required. Optional integrations need their own addons/libraries installed separately.

Start with inexpensive test items. New installations have no transfer rules enabled.
Snapshots are account/world-specific observations. Offline characters cannot move items remotely.
Planned deliveries require source and recipient bank visits. Unknown recipes cannot be gathered.
After manually crafting a gathered recipe, release its ingredient hold under Requests / release.
Inspect uncertain transfers before cancelling or replacing them; cancellation never reverses completed moves.

Use Log > Report for a redacted troubleshooting report. Do not share full SavedVariables or private inventory screenshots publicly.
