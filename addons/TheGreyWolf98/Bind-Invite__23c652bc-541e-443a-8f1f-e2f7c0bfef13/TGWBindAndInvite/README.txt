Bind and Invite 1.0.2 — Xbox
Author: @TheGreyWolf98

Upload TGWBindAndInvite.addon through the Bethesda add-on uploader. The internal folder and saved-variable names are retained so existing settings carry over.

COMMANDS
/bi opens settings.
/bind opens the manual binding preview.
/invite opens settings with Whisper invites selected.
/invite @name preserves normal invitations.
/bi keyword yourword sets a custom whisper keyword (1–32 characters).
/bi socials opens the social page. /bi support also works.
/tgw and /tgwbi remain available as legacy menu aliases.

CONTROLLER
LB/RB switch Settings, Preview, Help and Log.
D-pad selects settings or preview items; left/right switch settings columns.
A changes a setting or includes/excludes a preview piece.
X opens Socials from Settings, Help or Log. In Preview, X arms the batch; press X again within 10 seconds to bind the included pieces.
Y rescans the backpack. LT/RT change pages. B closes, or returns from Socials.

BINDING
Automatic binding starts OFF. Manual binding uses a reviewed preview and two presses of X. Lowest-quality duplicates are chosen. Locked, stolen, collected and ineligible pieces are skipped. Binding ends trading rights; lock gear in Inventory to protect it. Turning automatic binding on affects newly received eligible gear only, not gear already in your backpack. Turning it off cancels queued binding. Combat stops binding.

INVITES
Automatic invites start OFF. Enable Whisper invites and choose a keyword and 4- or 12-player limit. Senders must whisper the exact keyword in ESO chat; case and outer spaces are ignored. Xbox messages do not count. You must be solo or group leader. Recipients still accept invitations. Pending invites reserve places for 30 seconds, repeated senders have a 30-second cooldown, and requests are limited to one per second. Disable competing invite features if they conflict.
