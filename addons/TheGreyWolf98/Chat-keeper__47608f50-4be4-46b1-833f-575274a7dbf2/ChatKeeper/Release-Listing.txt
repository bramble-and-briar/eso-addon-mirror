OVERVIEW
Keep the conversation going when you switch characters or reload your UI.
Chat Keeper saves recent player chat, adds optional timestamps and lets you
move the native HUD chat box to a more comfortable position—all with a
transparent background and controller-friendly settings.

DESCRIPTION
Chat Keeper keeps a shared chat history across your characters on the same
account and server, making it easier to pick up a conversation after a toon
swap or UI reload.

Features
- Saves supported player conversations, including guild, group and whispers.
- Keeps the latest 100 messages during your current session.
- Restores 25–100 recent messages after a quick login or UI reload.
- Clears saved chat after 30 minutes offline, so yesterday's conversations do not return.
- Quiet periods while online do not expire your conversation.
- Optional timestamps in 12-hour or 24-hour format.
- Move native HUD chat horizontally or vertically to suit your interface.
- Transparent HUD with no custom artwork or background panels.
- Controller-friendly settings and a paged history viewer.
- No external addon libraries required.

Type /chatkeeper for settings or /chatkeeper history to browse saved chat.
Use /chatkeeper reset to restore the original HUD position.

Saved messages are restored locally and marked [Saved]; they are never sent
to anyone. Saved links become plain text, while new live messages retain
normal ESO links. Chat Keeper only records messages received while running;
it cannot capture conversations while you are offline. Replayed messages use
local system lines rather than their original channel filters.
