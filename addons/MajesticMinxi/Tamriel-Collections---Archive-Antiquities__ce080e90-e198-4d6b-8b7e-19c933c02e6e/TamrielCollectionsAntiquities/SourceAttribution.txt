Antiquity acquisition data attribution

The acquisition facts in AntiquitySources.lua were adapted and condensed from
UESP's Mythic Item pages and contributors, accessed 5 November 2026.
AntiquityMotifs.lua adapts motif lead sources from the zone pages below;
its verified lead ranges compact 98 chapter records into five ranges.

pages below. Four unverified sources are omitted. Conflicting source IDs for
Smoothed Stone Grinder (502), Fargrave (547) and West Weald (687) are corrected
using each furnishing's Antiquity entry:
