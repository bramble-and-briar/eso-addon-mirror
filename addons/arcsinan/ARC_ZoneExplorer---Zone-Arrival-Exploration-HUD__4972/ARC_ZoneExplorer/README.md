# ARC_ZoneExplorer

**ARC_ZoneExplorer** by **@arcsinan** is a lightweight, high-performance ESO addon that displays an elegant, modern arrival dashboard whenever you enter a new zone or city in Tamriel (upon finishing a loading screen).

---

## Features

- **Zone Guide Overview**:
  - ⚡ **Skyshards**: Discovered vs. total in current zone.
  - 🗺️ **Wayshrines**: Discovered vs. total.
  - 🏰 **Delves & Public Dungeons**: Completed bosses.
  - 💀 **World Bosses**: Group bosses defeated.
  - 🌀 **World Events**: Dolmens / Geysers / Harrowstorms closed.
  - 📍 **Striking Locales & Points of Interest**: Discovered.
  - 📜 **Zone Story Quests**: Completed main story chapters.
- **Item Set Collections (Stickerbook)**:
  - Automatically queries the zone's overland item sets and displays collection progress (e.g. *Mother's Sorrow: 38/45*).
- **Daily Reminders & Timers**:
  - 🐎 **Riding Training**: Instant notification if training is ready, remaining cooldown time, or maxed out (60/60/60).
  - 🔨 **Daily Crafting Writs**: Status of active daily writ quests.
- **Smart Auto-Dismiss & Fadeout**:
  - Smoothly appears on zone entry, displays for your chosen duration (default: 8 seconds), and fades out automatically.
  - Can also be toggled anytime with `/zoneexplorer` or `/aze`.
  - Fully movable: click and drag anywhere on the window; position is saved per account.

---

## Slash Commands

- `/zoneexplorer` or `/aze` - Toggle the Zone Explorer HUD on/off.
- `/azesettings` - Open the Add-on Settings configuration panel.
- `/arcdonate` - Open in-game mail to send gold or feedback to @arcsinan (PC/EU).

---

## Dependencies (Mandatory)

- **LibAddonMenu-2.0** (`>= 43`): Required for the settings configuration panel.
