Welcome to AutoNomNom

Are you tired of feeling hungry and thirsty in the middle of a trial? Do you have multiple different food/drinks that you use depending on role? Have you tried those addons that nag you, but you don't like being nagged? Have you tried those addons that eat for you, but can't be bothered telling the addon what food you are using at the moment? Then AutoNomNom might just be for you (although given how picky you are about other addons, that is probably wishful thinking on my part). 

What it does:
1. When you consume food or drink, this addon will remember what food/drink you consumed.
2. When that food or drink buff is about to expire, the addon will simply eat or drink another portion of it for you.
3. You can turn this all on/off in the settings, or via the handy hotkey.


You don't have to do anything - just make sure autoconsume is ON in the settings, and let your inner gourmet eat what they want, safe in the knowledge that this addon won't let you go hungry or thirsty again (unless you run out of food, or toggle it off, or your computer breaks or something).


Why THIS addon?
You don't have to remember to 'autoconsume' something - you just eat and forget about it - which means you You can set a food in (say) wizard's wardrobe and AutoNomNom will just maintain that for you until you change to a different food.
There is a hotkey that will quickly toggle the auto-eat on, but the learning is ALWAYS on. So just eat as you go and hit the hotkey if you want to make sure you don't run out before the end of the trial etc.
It will warn you if you are running low.
It's friendly!
