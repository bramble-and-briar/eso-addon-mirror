# Lethal PVP Insights (ESO)
by **Lethal2k**

## Install
Install it with **Minion**, or download it from [ESOUI](https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5037-LethalPvPInsights.html) or esopvp.com. To install by hand, copy the `LethalPVPInsights` folder to `Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns/`, then enable it in the in-game AddOns menu.

**Updating from PVP Data Insight (1.9.58 or older):** delete the old `AddOns/PVPDataInsight` folder, unzip the new one, then start `LPI COMPANION.bat` once with the game closed: it moves your saved data (`SavedVariables/PVPDataInsight.lua`) to the renamed addon.
In **Settings > Social**, turn on the **PvP kill feed**. The kill feed is the main data source for kills, assists and the Kill Feed tab.

## Settings
**Settings > Add-Ons > Lethal PVP Insights** (requires [LibAddonMenu-2.0](https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu.html)). `/pvpdi settings` or the **Settings** button in the main window jumps straight there. In the Settings list, look for the second *Add-Ons* entry, near the bottom, which has a search box. If the panel doesn't appear, `/pvpdi lamcheck` shows what's missing. Every setting also has a `/pvpdi` command, so the addon works without LibAddonMenu too.

## Open
`/pvpdi`, or set a key under **Controls > Keybindings > Lethal PVP Insights**. Press ESC to close.

## Window
The tab's list is on the left. Click a player and their details appear on the right. **Skills** and **Gear & Sets** are shown side by side. Skills shows the front and back bar; for group members without captured gear, or for opponents, it lists the skills and procs seen in combat instead. Gear & Sets shows set piece counts with the traits on each set (e.g. *Divines 4, Infused 1*), then every item with its type (weapon, or armour weight and piece), trait and enchant, coloured by quality. Hover a skill or item for its tooltip; click it to keep the tooltip open. The window adjusts its height to your screen so a full build fits without scrolling. Click the next player to switch, with no going back. **Follow** (keep a snapshot of the player) and **Link** (share the build in chat) are in the details panel. When a character was seen under another @account before, the title shows *formerly @OldName*.

## Tabs
- **My Kills**: your killing blows and assists, with a count for each enemy @account and class (one player's Dragonknight and Sorcerer are separate rows; the Character column lists that class's characters). A gold **Total** row at the top adds up what's listed (period, faction filter and search). Only kills the kill feed shows as killed by you count as killing blows, so turn on the PvP kill feed (without it, everything you helped kill counts as an assist). Duels are the exception: the kill feed doesn't report them, so winning a duel counts as a killing blow and losing one counts as being killed (a forfeit counts as neither). Each enemy death counts once: an assist is upgraded to a killing blow when the kill feed names you as the killer (chat shows *corrected*). Periods: Session, Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, This Month, Last Month, Lifetime. A day runs from 03:00 UTC to 03:00 UTC (the game's daily reset), whatever your time zone. The **Reset** button under the list clears the period you're viewing, for example *Reset Today*. Every period except Session asks you to confirm first. *Reset Lifetime* clears every period of that tab. The Kill Feed tab has the same button.
- **Kill Feed**: a leaderboard of every kill seen in the kill feed, by @account and class, over the same periods. Use the **Faction** button to switch between All, AD, EP and DC. Click a name to see what you recorded when you targeted that player, friend or foe: class, race, faction, CP, health, mundus and buffs. Minor/Major buffs and debuffs aren't listed. Buffs marked with a **\*** (such as Radiating Regeneration, Echoing Vigor, Healing Burst) may have been cast on them by another player. Buffs are grouped the same way as an enemy's skills (Skill, Passive Skill, Ultimate, Proc Set, **Mythic**, Weapon Glyph, CP, **Food Buff**, **XP Buff**, other Buff). Immunities, Elder Scroll buffs, Crux/Empower/Sunderer, Dodge Fatigue and wards or burst shields (which may come from other players) are left out. Right-click a row to pick its type from a menu or hide it; these choices only affect the Buffs list, not Opponents or Group. Each time you target the player, newly seen buffs are added to the list (no duplicates); after 36 hours the next look starts a fresh list (change it in Settings or with `/pvpdi buffhours <1-168>`).
- **Opponents**: enemies who attacked you in Cyrodiil, Imperial City or a Battleground, or your duel partner during a duel: one row per player and class over the last 7 days (Days = on how many days). In the details, the **Days** buttons show one day at a time (a build can change between days) and **Other classes** switches to the same player's other classes. Group members and players from your own alliance in Cyrodiil are never listed. NPCs are kept out, because a name is only listed once the addon knows its @account. Buffs only count from someone who has already damaged you. For each one you get faction, class, max health and mundus. Click a player to see what they hit you with, grouped in this order: **Weapon** (light and heavy attacks, which show their weapon type), **Skill**, **Passive Skill**, **Ultimate**, **Synergy**, **Proc Set**, **Weapon Glyph**, **Weapon Poison**, **CP Damage**, then **Damage Effect** (Burning, Chilled, Poisoned, Sundered, Bash and similar). Buffs, debuffs, damage shields (Barrier, Bone Wall and others) and siege weapons (including Fire/Frost/Lightning Lancer) are hidden. If a row is in the wrong group, right-click it and pick the right type from the menu; *Hide this row* removes it, and *Back to automatic* undoes your choice. The addon remembers your choices, and `/pvpdi skillreset` clears them. Health is the reading taken when you targeted the player; implausible readings are ignored. Data older than 7 days is deleted automatically.
The **Follow** button (anywhere in the details) follows that @account; on a player you follow it says **Unfollow**, which stops following them and removes their snapshots.
- **Following**: everyone you follow. Press **Follow** once and the addon keeps a **daily snapshot** of that player for as long as you follow them: every day you meet them (in combat, by targeting them or in your group), that day's snapshot is written again with everything recorded so far that day, including captured gear and skill bars, so it keeps growing during the day. With uploads to esopvp.com on, what other players recorded about them arrives with the shared data, also one entry per day (marked with a blue **+**). There is one row per player and class (Days = on how many days, Last seen = the newest day); in the details, the **day buttons** show one day at a time, and the class buttons switch to the same player's other classes. The Type column shows Enemy, Player or Group. Automatic snapshots are kept 30 days; followed players don't count toward the Group tab's 200 limit.
- **Group**: the last 200 accounts you grouped with in PvP (Settings: *Group history: record groups in* PvP, PvE or Both; `/pvpdi groupscope pvp|pve|both`). To drop one player, select them and press **Remove**, or right-click their row. For each one: class, race, CP, role, faction, max health, mundus and permanent buffs, plus every skill or proc the addon saw them use while grouped with you (damage, heals, buffs). Click a player to see their build, then press **Follow** to keep a snapshot. **Clear Group History**, at the bottom of the list, deletes everyone in the tab together with their imported gear. It asks you to confirm first, and followed players (Following tab) are kept.

Skills marked with a **\*** may come from another player: a skill seen only as a buff on them when their skill bars were captured without it, and group-buff skills such as *... Contingency* and *... Banner* unless they're on their bars. When their gear was captured, sets seen only as buffs on them (another member's Powerful Assault, Pearlescent Ward...) are left out, and the sets they wear are listed.

## Real Death Recap
Off by default: tick **Real Death Recap** in Settings. It only runs in PvP areas (Cyrodiil, Imperial City, Battlegrounds). When you die, a window shows:
- the **top 5 damage sources** of that fight: skill and player, damage, hits and share. An attack's part absorbed by your damage shield counts with it (*Molten Whip 10,000 (3,000 absorbed)*); a skill that only ever hit your shield is left out of the top 5;
- the **last 10 hits** before death, with time before death, crit, blocked, *absorbed by shield* and the killing blow.

`/pvpdi recap` shows the last recap again. Esc, Alt or the Close button closes it.

## Build links in chat
Select a player and press **Link** (or `/pvpdi link`): a *[LPI build: @Name]* link goes into the chat input. Anyone with Lethal PVP Insights can click it to see that build (class, race, CP, role, mundus, skill bars or skills seen, gear).

## esopvp.com (data sharing)
The LPI Companion can upload your LPI data to **esopvp.com**: tick **Upload my LPI data to esopvp.com** in the Companion window (untick it any time). Nothing leaves your PC until you tick it.
- When: after each /reloadui, logout or exit (that is when the game writes `SavedVariables\LethalPVPInsights.lua`). The Companion uploads that file a few seconds later; the server keeps only the LPI data it needs.
- **Identifier**: shown in the Companion (Copy button) and in the addon settings under Data sharing (after /reloadui). Enter it when you register on esopvp.com to see your own data in the browser. **New Identifier** makes the old one stop working.
- **Upload, then clear local data** (Settings > Data, or `/pvpdi clearlocal confirm`) saves and uploads everything, then clears it from this PC once esopvp.com confirmed it (usually at the next /reloadui): kill counts, kill feed, opponents, group history and friendly players. Everything stays on esopvp.com, including followed players you unfollow later. Followed players and settings are kept. `/pvpdi clearlocal cancel` stops a pending clear.
- **Shared data**: after each upload the Companion downloads `LPI_Shared.lua` (players other uploaders saw in the last 7 days, as their sharing settings allow; players with sets and the most skills/buffs first). After /reloadui the details pane adds what you didn't record yourself, marked with a blue **+**. Only data of the same character from the same day is combined (a build can change between days). For players you follow, the shared data is kept in your SavedVariables, so it stays even when a newer download no longer lists them.
- **Clear collected data** in the Companion clears the same data from this PC the next time the addon loads (/reloadui or log in), without waiting for an upload. Followed players and settings are kept; what was already uploaded stays on esopvp.com. To delete your uploaded data, use esopvp.com > My account.
- **Settings > Data sharing** decides who may see each kind: *Share Group Builds*, *Share Followed Players* and *Share Friendly Players*, each **All**, **Your Faction**, **Guild Members** (tick which of your guilds), **Friends** or **None**. Defaults: Friendly Players **Your Faction**, Group Builds and Followed Players **Friends**. Opponent data and the kill feed are always used for public stats.
- For these rules the addon also saves your alliance per character, your guild names and your friends list (the server keeps friends only as hashes). Duel wins are logged as kill events marked as duels; they never count in public stats.

## Kill event log
Besides the Kill Feed counters, every kill feed message is stored as its own record, so kills can later be matched across players (same campaign, killer, victim and location, times within a few seconds). Each record has: time (UTC seconds), megaserver, campaign (id and name), zone, the kill feed's location name and whether the game flagged it as the kill spot, the killer's and victim's @account, character, alliance and rank, and your own map position and location when the message arrived. A repeated message for the same kill is stored once. Records are kept 7 days by default (Settings, or `/pvpdi killdays <1-60>`); `/pvpdi status` shows how many are stored.

## Gear and skill bars (encounter log)
Addons can't read another player's gear or skill bars directly, but the game's encounter log can. The addon and a small desktop **Companion** handle the whole process:

1. **Start the Companion once** by double-clicking **LPI COMPANION.bat** in the addon folder. You don't need to install anything. It uses Python 3 if you have it, and otherwise the PowerShell edition that comes with Windows 10 and 11. Either way it runs without a console window; only the Companion window (with the LPI shield icon) appears. The first start also adds a **LPI Companion** shortcut to your desktop and makes it start with Windows (both can be switched off in its window), and puts a **Lethal PVP Insights Companion** shortcut with the LPI icon in the addon folder. It finds the addon folder from the Encounter.log path by itself, and imports captured fights automatically. Big logs are imported in the background, so the window never freezes.
   **The Companion must be running before you click Upload in game**, otherwise the browser only shows "can't be reached". The in-game import window shows whether the Companion was running when the UI last loaded.
2. **Play as usual.** When you're grouped in Cyrodiil, Imperial City or a Battleground and some online members have no gear data yet, the addon turns the encounter log on by itself.
3. **The addon decides when it has enough.** The game writes each member's gear and both skill bars into the log when you enter combat. On each combat start, everyone still missing who is in group support range counts as captured. Once nobody is left, the log turns off a few seconds later, so the file stays small. If someone was out of range, it waits for the next fight. It also turns off after 10 minutes, or when you leave the group or the PvP area.
4. **Upload.** When you're out of combat, the in-game import window opens with the log path and a list of who was captured. Click **Upload** and confirm the game's "open link" prompt. The Companion imports the log, and a browser tab shows the result. You can also click **Import** in the Companion window, which pops up at the same time, or tick *Import automatically*. The **Import Log** button in the main window opens this window at any time.
5. **Optional: delete the log.** Encounter.log isn't needed after an import. Use **Delete Log** in the in-game import window, *Delete Encounter.log* in Settings, `/pvpdi deletelog`, or *Delete log now* in the Companion. To have it deleted after every import, tick *Delete log after import* in the Companion. Imported gear is kept either way. The Companion only deletes Encounter.log on its own when every logging session in it was started by LPI. If the log also contains logging you started yourself (with `/encounterlog`, or one that was already running when the Companion started), it asks first: a Yes/No box in the Companion, or a *Delete Encounter.log anyway* button on the browser page. With *Delete log after import*, such a log is kept, and deleted later once LPI's own capture is confirmed after a /reloadui.
6. Click **Reload UI**. In the Group tab, players now show **yes** in the Gear column. Click one to see their sets (piece counts, with back-bar pieces shown separately, and the traits on each set), front and back bars, and every item's type, trait, enchant and quality. Hover a row for its tooltip; click it to keep the tooltip open. **Build 1/N** switches between different setups a player used.

Settings: `/pvpdi autolog auto|ask|never` sets whether the addon turns the log on automatically (the default), asks first, or never does. Players already captured aren't logged again until you import, or for 2 hours if you don't. New members who join later are captured in a new round after a short pause. `/pvpdi popup on|off` turns the in-game import window on or off. `/pvpdi logpath <path>` is for when your Documents folder is somewhere else, such as OneDrive. `/pvpdi capture [seconds|off]` turns the log on manually. The addon never switches off a log that you turned on yourself with `/encounterlog`.

Enemies who show up in the log get their gear and skill bars in the Opponents tab too. **Follow** stores the gear and bars with the snapshot, so they stay after the importer drops old players. The importer keeps the 200 most recently seen accounts and only reads what was added to the log since the last run.

Imported gear and skill bars are also kept in the addon's saved data for 7 days after they were captured, so they stay in the Group tab even if you delete Encounter.log or the Companion's import history is reset. An anonymous player stays matched to the account they were first matched to.

Without encounter-log data, a build only shows what the game exposes and what the addon has seen the player cast. If you regroup with someone more than an hour later, recording starts fresh for them.

Max health and mundus are recorded when you target the enemy with your reticle.

## New versions
After the Companion's download from esopvp.com, the addon tells you in chat (and next to the window title) when a newer version is available.
