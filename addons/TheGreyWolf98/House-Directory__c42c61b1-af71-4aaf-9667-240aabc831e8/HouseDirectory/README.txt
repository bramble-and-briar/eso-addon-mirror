House Directory 1.0.0
By @TheGreyWolf98

Open with /houses (or /house). No external dependencies.
LB/RB: switch Owned / Unowned.
A on a Staple, Classic or Notable heading: expand/collapse its houses.
Hold D-pad up/down: move. LT/RT: jump a page. B: close.
Select a house for its picture, nickname and furnishing details.
Owned houses: right-stick click edits your personal nickname/storage note.
X travels inside. Y prepares your account-specific house link; open text chat
normally, choose a channel and send it yourself. Visitors need your permissions.
Unowned houses: X previews. No share or purchase action. The catalogue includes
houses that are not currently for sale; ESO may restrict some previews.

Notes and visit snapshots are shared across characters on the same server.
Official names stay visible. ESO collectible nicknames appear when no custom note
exists. Custom notes are local to this addon and do not rename the house in ESO.

Visit your own house once to record usage. Counts refresh every five seconds while
inside. Standard usage, total capacity and free slots appear in the list; all four
furnishing categories appear in details. The bar represents standard furnishing
usage and turns red at 95% full. Saved counts are labelled and are not live remote
readings. Unvisited houses show Visit to scan, never an assumed zero. Revisit to
refresh counts, especially after changes to furnishings or ESO Plus membership.

Uses ESO's built-in house images, where available. No bundled image downloads.
The catalogue scans registered house IDs 1-2000 in small batches, roughly two
seconds after loading. A future game update may require extending this range.

Xbox testing confirmed previews, nickname editing, furnishing snapshots,
owned-house chat links, and persistence after travel/reload. The final warm styling
and capacity bar passed local checks; visual appearance should be checked on Xbox.
