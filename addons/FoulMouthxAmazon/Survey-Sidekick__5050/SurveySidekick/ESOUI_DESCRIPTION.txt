**Survey Sidekick**
**By FoulMouthxAmazon**

Your surveys are piling up. Your crafting bag demands tribute. Let’s turn that backlog into a haul!

Survey Sidekick organizes your crafting surveys, helps you travel to their locations, and tracks the materials you collect along the way.

**Optional Addons**

- [**LibTreasure**](https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info3227-LibTreasure.html) — Enables survey locations, map waypoints, and wayshrine selection.
- [**Tamriel Trade Centre**](https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info1245-TamrielTradeCentre.html) — Provides estimated material values when price data is available.

Gathering totals and collection goals work without either addon. Install optional addons and their dependencies separately.

**Features**

- **Organized survey list:** View backpack surveys by crafting type, with duplicate copies combined into one entry.
- **Travel tools:** Place a map waypoint, preview a destination and recall cost, or travel to the closest unlocked wayshrine on the survey’s map.
- **Live harvest tracking:** Track material quantities and gathering time with Start, Pause, and Resume controls.
- **Collection goals:** Choose a survey type, select a material by name, and enter your target quantity. No item IDs needed!
- **The Hoard Report:** Save gathering sessions, track your biggest haul, and copy summaries or detailed material lists.
- **Estimated values:** See available price estimates alongside clearly marked unpriced quantities.
- **Compact interface:** Resize the window or minimize it to a live tracker bar. Window position and visibility are remembered per character.
- **Color choices:** Click Colors in the title bar for six presets or separate background, title bar and border choices. Colors apply instantly and save per character.

**Getting Started**

1. Type `/surveys` to open the addon.
2. Open **Current Run** and click **Start / Resume**.
3. Gather materials.
4. Click **Finish Run** to view **The Hoard Report**.

For survey travel, select a survey under **My Surveys**, then use the map, wayshrine, or gold-coin preview icon.

Open **Help** or type `/surveys help` for instructions.

**Useful Commands**

- `/surveys` — Open or close the window.
- `/surveys colors` - Open the appearance menu.
- `/surveyport` — Travel to the selected survey’s closest unlocked wayshrine.
- `/surveys goal` — Open collection goal setup.
- `/surveys scale 70` — Make the window smaller.
- `/surveys scale 80` — Restore the default size.

**Things to Know**

All supported crafting materials looted during an active run count, including surveys, regular nodes, containers, and creature drops. The Craft Bag can remain enabled.

The **Regular-sized / Survey-Large** breakdown is an estimate. Other unusually large drops may appear under Survey/Large. Bank quantities are last-seen snapshots refreshed while your bank is open.

Normal ESO travel costs and restrictions apply. Price estimates are not guaranteed sale prices.
