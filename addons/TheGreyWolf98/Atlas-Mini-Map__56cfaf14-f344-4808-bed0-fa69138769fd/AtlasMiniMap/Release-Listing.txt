Atlas Mini Map 1.0.3
by @TheGreyWolf98

Readable north-up minimap with large clock, FPS, latency, native services,
journal quest icons, optional Atlas pins, waypoints and antiquity search areas.
Adjust size, position and zoom through /minimap. No external libraries.

UPDATE 1.0.3
Compact clock header directly above the map; FPS and latency moved to footer.
Added native service/trader and journal quest/breadcrumb icons. Native icons
now remain enabled alongside Atlas layers, with duplicate icons removed.
Dig areas match the main map's colours, shading and textured border.

Scrying overlays show only search areas exposed by ESO on the current map.
Group markers and arbitrary third-party custom pins are not included.
