Set Hunter 1.1.0 - by brianmit
=================================

Where every set drops (down to the boss for monster helms), your set collection
progress and a farming wishlist, plus an XP farming guide.

OPEN IT
  Click the "Set Hunter" button (top left), or type /sethunter (or /sh), or use
  the keybind. The button hides during combat and can be made compact (only the
  shield) in the settings. Your inventory and bank also get a small Set Hunter
  shield that opens My items right next to your bag.

SETS
  Current location ..  what drops where you are right now
  Collection overview  how far your Set Collection is, per category
  Almost complete  ..  sets you're 1 to 3 pieces from finishing
  Recently collected   pieces you added to your collection lately, with where
  Crafted sets  .....  every craftable set, its station, and whether this
                       character has enough traits researched to make it
  Wishlist  .........  sets you want, where they drop and the pieces you own
      By place  .....  where your wishes drop, the place with the most first,
                       with travel / queue right there (plan your farming)
      Whole sets  ...  sets you want complete
      Wanted pieces .  sets where you only want certain pieces (Head, Chest...)
      Completed  ....  wishes you've fully collected
  Trait hunt  .......  sets you want in certain traits, and which of those
                       traits you already have
  Dungeons, Trials, Arenas, Overland, PvP, Other
                       every place, with its sets and how they drop
  Monster helms  ....  every monster set with the boss that drops the head

MY ITEMS
  My items  ........  every set item you own: all characters, bank, house storage,
                       with trait, quality, level and where it is
  Not in collection yet
                       pieces you own that aren't in your Set Collection
                       (bind them to unlock)
  Safe to deconstruct
                       bound pieces already in your Set Collection (you can
                       reconstruct them), not worn and not a trait you hunt
  Duplicates  ......  the same piece (set, slot, trait) owned more than once
  Tradeable  .......  pieces that aren't bound yet (sell, trade, mail)
  Below max level  .  pieces under CP 160
  By trait, By slot, By quality, By gear type, By set type
                       your pieces grouped; pick a group in the list
  The character picker at the top shows all characters, one character, the bank or
  house storage, with how many set pieces each has.
  At the bank, the window opens on My items: click a bank piece to find it.

FCO ITEMSAVER (optional)
  With FCO ItemSaver installed, right-click any of your pieces to set its marks
  (lock, sell, deconstruct...) right in the menu, also for pieces on other
  characters: the mark shows on the piece next time you log in there. On
  Duplicates, "Keep the best, mark the other N for deconstruction" keeps the best
  copy and marks the rest. Pieces locked in FCO ItemSaver are never "safe to
  deconstruct". Log in on each character once after installing FCO ItemSaver.

ITEM TOOLTIPS
  The game's own item tooltips (bag, bank, worn gear, crafting, loot) get a few
  Set Hunter lines: on your wishlist, a trait you hunt, not in your Set Collection
  yet, and how many more copies you own and where. Can be turned off in the settings.

  Star  ............  add to / remove from wishlist
  Double-click  ....  jump to where the set drops
  Right-click  .....  the piece on top, then Wishlist (Hunt traits..., Wanted
                       pieces...), Find it (where it drops, queue / travel) and
                       This piece (show in bag, FCO ItemSaver marks, link in chat)
  Hover  ...........  item tooltip + where it drops + which pieces you still miss
                       + the pieces you own

DUNGEONS
  "Queue dungeon" (or right-click > Queue for this dungeon...) opens the queue
  window: pick Normal / Veteran and Damage / Healer / Tank, then Queue. It queues
  you for that exact dungeon in the Activity Finder, shows the level / CP needed,
  and warns you when a monster helm needs Veteran. Other places use "Travel there".

XP FARMING
  Grind spots, XP boosts, and sets / skills / consumables for grinding.
  Double-click a grind spot to travel there: you're sent to the wayshrine closest
  to the spot (/sethunter travelcheck lists the wayshrine for every place).
  Every XP page shows your level (or CP) and how far you are to the next one.
  This session  .....  XP earned, XP per hour, and when you'll reach your next
                       level or champion point at this pace
  Daily bonuses  ....  the once-a-day Group Finder rewards: which are still open
                       today and how much XP they give
  XP boosts shows how long your Experience Scroll / Ambrosia still lasts.

RUN SUMMARY
  After a dungeon (or when you leave one with loot), a small window shows the
  time, the set pieces you got, how many were new to your collection, your runs
  today and your collection progress, with Queue again / See loot / Close.
  Turn it off with right-click on the Set Hunter button.
  /sethunter testrun shows a preview.

WISHLIST ALERTS
  When you loot a piece from a wishlist set, a message shows on your screen
  (with a sound) and in chat: "Wanted piece!" or "Hunted trait!" when it's one
  you picked. /sethunter testdrop shows a preview.
  When you enter a place where a wishlist set drops, it's said in chat.

SETTINGS
  Settings > Addons > Set Hunter, click the emblem at the top left of the window,
  right-click the Set Hunter button > Settings, or /sethunter settings.

NEEDS
  LibAddonMenu-2.0 (required).
  LibItemSets (recommended) for the set lists.
  Monster helms and the XP guide work without it.
  FCO ItemSaver (optional) for marking pieces from Set Hunter.

CHAT COMMANDS
  /sethunter help   shows all commands in chat
