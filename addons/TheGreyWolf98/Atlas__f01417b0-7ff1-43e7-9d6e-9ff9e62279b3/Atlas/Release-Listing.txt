ATLAS
By @TheGreyWolf98

Switchable map pins for skyshards, Shalidor lorebooks, treasure-map digs, surveys, bosses, delves, events, wayshrines, landmarks, Mundus stones and crafting stations. Now includes separate treasure chest and Thieves Guild trove layers.

Faster progressive loading shares map scans and keeps work within per-tick limits. Optional neon colours, larger symbols, hide-collected and backpack-only map/survey filters.

Chest and trove pins show recorded spawn sites, not live availability. Atlas can remember new sites when you interact with them. Coverage varies; troves require the relevant in-game access.

Open /atlas. D-pad selects, A toggles, Y refreshes, X opens Socials / Support and B closes. Existing settings carry over. No external libraries required.
