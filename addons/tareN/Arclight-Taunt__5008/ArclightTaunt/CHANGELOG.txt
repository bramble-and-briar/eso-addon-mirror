ARCLIGHT TAUNT - CHANGELOG

VERSION 1.4.0 | Interface & Quality of Life Update | 24 November 2026

INTERFACE & SETTINGS

- Reorganized the settings by task. The test is at the top of Boss warnings, the preview is easy to find under Crosshair, and boss detection is now under Targets & markers.
- A one-time chat tip shows first-time users where to find the addon settings.
- Shortened settings labels and help in English, German, French, Spanish and Russian. In German and French, the list footer now uses the singular when exactly one enemy is hidden.

COMBAT HUD & WARNINGS

- Improved countdown readability in High contrast for both narrow and full-row bars, including the final zero. Boss skulls and ESO target markers have more space between them, and the two warning banners align their skulls and text.
- Boss warnings adjust their position when a short window or high UI scale leaves less room on screen.
- Live boss warnings and cleanup continue while the taunt list preview is paused. A confirmed death clears only that boss from a shared expiration warning, even if its row has already gone.
- Boss detection now considers every boss slot provided by ESO.

TRACKING & COMPATIBILITY

- Delayed updates could bring back an ended taunt or cut a refreshed one short. Timers now ignore those outdated updates. Incomplete updates preserve the known enemy name and start time. Duplicate expiry updates no longer extend the brief display at zero.
- Switching aim to a player no longer lets a delayed update clear the enemy’s active taunt.
- Reassigning an ESO target marker clears it from the previous enemy even if a customized icon can’t be retrieved. Reapplying the enabled marker setting preserves markers already seen on other enemies.
- Incomplete crosshair settings restore missing values for the selected shape. Invalid media and translations fall back to built-in media and English, respectively. Existing valid settings carry over.

PERFORMANCE

- Reduced repeated checks when observing the target under the crosshair and skipped redundant sorting when an unpinned list contains only expired taunts already in order.
