## Author: Lethal2k
## Version: 1.10.5
## AddOnVersion: 285

## Description: Tracks your PvP kills/assists, kill-feed leaderboards per faction, and what enemy players hit you with (skills, procs, faction, max health, mundus), plus a history of players you grouped with and their builds (gear and skill bars via the encounter log importer). Also PvP alerts, group leader marker, FPS limits and performance boost per zone, quickslot keybinds, a skill blocker and auto quest hand-in. Configure via Settings > Lethal PVP Insights (/lpi settings jumps there) or /lpi.
