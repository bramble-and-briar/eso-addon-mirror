## About OneCrosshair

OneCrosshair adds animated crosshairs and compact combat indicators to ESO's standard PC interface. Keep resources and ability timing in view around your crosshair.

- Five crosshair presets: Dot, Large Dots, Rays, Diamonds and ESO Default.
- A resource ring for health, magicka and stamina, with a damage shield indicator.
- Global cooldown, heavy attack, cast and channel progress indicators.
- Low resource warnings and optional crosshair pulses on successful direct hits.
- Always visible, combat only, dynamic and hidden HUD modes.
- Adjustable crosshair and HUD opacity, resource ring size, arc length and line thickness.
- A live preview for crosshair presets and indicators.
