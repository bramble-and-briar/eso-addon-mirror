# Yudo's Inventory Processor
*Version:* 1.12.1
*Author:* YudoAn
*License:* Artistic-2.0

## Description
Yudo's Inventory Processor is an essential quality-of-life addon for The Elder Scrolls Online.
It is designed to streamline your inventory management by automatically processing items whenever you interact with crafting stations, merchants, and bankers.

## Features
- Auto Deposit: Deposits and stacks crafting materials and currency into your bank when interacting with a banker.
- Auto Refine: Refines raw materials at the relevant crafting station.
- Auto Fillet Fish: Fillets fish when you visit a provisioning station.
- Auto Deconstruct: Deconstructs items according to your custom settings when you visit a deconstructor or crafting station.
- Auto Repair: Repairs all gear when you visit a merchant.
- Auto Sell Junk: Sells junk to merchants, fences stolen junk 
- Auto Mark Junk: Items are marked as junk based on your settings upon looting or scanning, with options to fine-tune and reset junk memory.

Compatible with FCO ItemSaver and Dolgubon's Lazy Writ Crafter.

## Settings
Fully configurable via the in-game Addon Settings menu:
`ESC Menu -> Settings -> Addons -> Yudo's Inventory Processor`

## Installation
1. Download the latest release (zip file).
2. Extract the folder `YudosInventoryProcessor` to your Elder Scrolls Online AddOns directory:
   - **PC:** `Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns/`
3. Launch the game and enable the addon in the **Addons** menu.
4. **Dependencies:** This addon requires LibAddonMenu-2.0. If you are missing this library, please download it from ESOUI.
