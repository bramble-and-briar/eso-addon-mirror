# Yudo's Power Stats

**Author:** YudoAn
**License:** Artistic-2.0
**Version:** 1.6.2

## Description
Yudo's Power Stats is a utility addon for The Elder Scrolls Online that displays and tracks your vital combat metrics in real-time.

## Features
- **Offensive Power:** Monitor your highest offensive power between Weapon and Spell Damage, alongside your Physical and Spell Penetration.
- **Defensive Power:** Monitor your Physical and Spell Resistances plus total Block Mitigation.
- **Critical Power:** Monitor your Critical Chance, Critical Damage and even Critical Resistance in real-time.
- **Peak stats:** Records highest values reached during combat. Hover over the Stats window to view.
- **Custom Thresholds:** Set custom thresholds for each stat. Values turn Gold when reaching your target benchmarks.
- **Customizable UI:** Select the stats you want to display. Adjust scale or background opacity. Lock the UI to prevent accidental movement during combat.

Note: Crit Damage and Penetration values are based on direct bonuses applied to your character and exclude other conditional modifiers.

## Settings
Fully configurable via the in-game Addon Settings menu:
`ESC Menu -> Settings -> Addons -> Yudo's Power Stats`

## Installation
1. Download the latest release (zip file).
2. Extract the folder `YudosPowerStats` to your Elder Scrolls Online AddOns directory:
   - **PC:** `Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns/`
3. Launch the game and enable the addon in the **Addons** menu.
4. **Dependencies:** This addon requires LibAddonMenu-2.0. If you are missing this library, please download it from ESOUI.
