Group Bearings
Author: absolventR3M

Know how far away every marked symbol is, the way the compass already
shows quest givers.

COMPASS
  Distance under symbols: the distance (for example 48m) shows under
  each target marker symbol on your compass.
  - Symbols on group members show their distance all the time.
  - A marked NPC (enemy, boss, any NPC) shows its distance while your
    crosshair is on it. The game shares an NPC's position with add-ons
    only then, and only at fairly close range.
  - Anything else you mark, including players outside your group, shows
    its distance while you face it (read from the compass itself). The
    game never shares those players' positions with add-ons.
  - When two symbols overlap, the closer one keeps the text.

GROUP MEMBERS (toggles, off by default)
  - Group member distances: each member's distance shows under their
    pin on your compass. The pins are the game's own, left exactly as
    they are. When members overlap, only the closest one in the pile
    shows a distance, and a marked symbol always keeps its text.
  - Beam over group members: a column of light over each group member
    who has no symbol on them. Member beam color: By role (tanks blue,
    healers green, damage red), a colour from the list, or Custom for
    any colour. A member with a symbol gets the symbol's beam instead
    (Symbols at your feet), and the leader gets the crown beam while
    that is on. Beam look and distances are under Arrows and beams.

CROWN (toggles, off by default)
  - Track the crown: the leader's distance shows under the game's own
    crown on your compass (even with member distances off). Nothing shows
    while you lead the group yourself; everything else works as usual,
    and group members running Group Bearings see your crown.
  - Beam over the crown and Arrow at your feet: a light beam over the
    leader and an arrow at your feet pointing to them, like Artaeum
    Group Tool's crown module. They work on their own, without Symbols
    at your feet.
  - Keep the crown when overlapping (on by default): the crown always
    stays visible on your compass. It draws over every compass marker
    it overlaps: symbols (a symbol on the leader too), quest and map
    pins, and other group members.
  - Crown always on top: the crown wins every overlap, on the compass
    and at your feet, no matter how far away it is (its icon, its
    distance and its arrow).
  - Crown color: the colour of the crown's beam and arrow. The crown on
    your compass always keeps the game's look.

SYMBOLS AT YOUR FEET (toggle, off by default)
  An arrow on the ground at your feet points toward each marked symbol,
  with the symbol and its distance, like the crown arrow in Artaeum
  Group Tool.
  - Works for marked group members all the time, and for a marked NPC
    while your crosshair is on it.
  - Arrows that would overlap: only the closest target's arrow shows.
    Arrow spacing sets how far apart (in degrees) arrows must be; raise
    it if arrows still crowd each other.
  - Light beam: a column of light over each marked symbol. Set its
    transparency, the distance it starts to show, the distance it stops
    showing, and whether it shows through walls. When your crosshair
    leaves a marked NPC, its beam stays on the spot you last saw it for
    10 seconds, fading out (the arrow goes right away, so it never points
    at an old spot).
  - The game may not draw arrows and beams inside dungeons and trials.
    The compass distances still work there.

PREVIEW
  Settings > Add-Ons > Group Bearings > Preview places test symbols and
  group members (with a crown) around you for 30 seconds. Close the
  menu and turn your camera to see the compass text, arrows and beams
  (turn on Beam over group members to see those too). Where typing in chat is available, /groupbearings preview does
  the same.

STATUS
  Settings > Add-Ons > Group Bearings > Advanced > Show status in chat
  prints what Group Bearings sees: every group member and their
  distance (or why they can't be tracked: offline, another zone, another
  instance), the crown, tracked symbols and which features are on.
  Handy when something does not show. Also /groupbearings status.

ACCOUNT-WIDE
  Settings are saved for your whole account: every character uses the
  same ones. The game switches add-ons on per character. To turn Group
  Bearings on for all characters at once, open Add-Ons from the
  character select screen (before logging in), where All Characters is
  selected, and enable Group Bearings and LibHarvensAddonSettings.

REQUIRES
  LibHarvensAddonSettings
  On console, install it from the in-game add-on browser.

SETUP
  1. Install Group Bearings and LibHarvensAddonSettings.
  2. Add-ons are switched on per character. Enable both from the
     character select screen's Add-Ons menu (All Characters) to turn
     them on everywhere at once, or on each character, then Reload UI.
  3. Open Settings > Add-Ons > Group Bearings to turn features on.
  4. Make sure the game's compass shows target markers
     (Settings > Interface > Compass Target Markers).
