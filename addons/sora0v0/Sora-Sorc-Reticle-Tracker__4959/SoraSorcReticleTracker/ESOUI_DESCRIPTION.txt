Sora Sorc Reticle Tracker

A compact reticle-side Sorcerer tracker designed to keep important combat information near the reticle.

Features
- Crystal Fragments proc timer on the left side of the reticle.
- Bound Armaments stack count on the right side of the reticle.
- Optional text-only Maelstrom Arena back-bar ground-skill timer below the reticle.
- Crystal Fragments works even while the skill is on the inactive weapon bar.
- Bound Armaments stack colors: 1-2 white, 3 gold, 4+ red.
- Maelstrom timer colors: white normally, gold at 5 seconds, red at 2 seconds.
- Independent position and size settings.
- Server-specific SavedVariables.

Optional Maelstrom timer supports:
- Merciless Charge / Perfected Merciless Charge + Stampede
- Crushing Wall / Perfected Crushing Wall + Elemental Blockade or Unstable Wall of Elements
- Thunderous Volley / Perfected Thunderous Volley + Endless Hail

Dependency
- LibAddonMenu-2.0

Development note: This addon was created with AI assistance. Code was reviewed and tested in game by sora0v0.
