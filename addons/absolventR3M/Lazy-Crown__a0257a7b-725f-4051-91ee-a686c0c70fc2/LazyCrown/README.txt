Lazy Crown
Author: absolventR3M

Too lazy to lead? Let the crown decide. In Cyrodiil, Lazy Crown picks
where to go next, and who's wearing the crown decides how it picks and
how it talks. Every call goes on the scoreboard. Nothing is ever sent
unless you press Send.

Every wheel entry, crown and option is explained on Lazy Crown's page
in the add-on browser, and in game under Main Menu > Add-Ons > Lazy
Crown.

SETUP
  1. Enable Lazy Crown, LibRadialMenu and LibHarvensAddonSettings in
     the Add-Ons menu, then Reload UI.
  2. Turn on HUD Chat Display in Settings > Social, or you won't see
     Lazy Crown's lines on screen.
  3. In the LibRadialMenu settings, set "Wheel Location" above 0.
  4. In Lazy Crown > Wheel slots, press "Put on". Skip LibRadialMenu's
     "Assign Slots" because it breaks whispers, group invites, Enter
     Now and respawning until you Reload UI. Same goes for "Defaults"
     or a color swatch in any add-on's settings.
  5. Open Text Chat yourself once each session (Main Menu > Text
     Chat) before using Crown Command, Scoreboard or Override. If an
     add-on opens it first, the game blocks Send until you Reload UI.
  6. Open your quickslot wheel, switch to "Addon Entries", and pick.

REQUIRES
  LibRadialMenu (version 9 or newer) and LibHarvensAddonSettings.
  Optional: LibGroupCombatStats, so Group Check can count the
  ultimates teammates share. Install them from the add-on browser.
