CHARACTER HOMES - HOW TO USE
============================

Character Homes keeps a list of the homes you want quick access to - your own,
your friends', and guild halls - and teleports you to them with one click or a
chat command.

You can use it two ways:
  - Open the Character Homes window with /homes
  - Type the slash commands (listed further down) in chat


SAVING A HOME
-------------

There are three kinds of saved home:
  - Primary   One per character. This is where plain /home (or /gohome)
              takes you.
  - Named     As many as you like, for the character you're playing.
  - Account   As many as you like, shared by every character on your account.

Easiest way: stand inside the house and type one of these
  /sethome                makes it this character's primary home
  /sethome <label>        saves it as a named home for this character
  /setglobalhome <label>  saves it as an account-wide home
(If you use Essential Housing Tools, type /setmyhome instead of /sethome -
see SLASH COMMANDS below.)

This works inside a friend's house too: it saves their house, and teleporting
to it later takes you to their house.

Saving a house that's already in that list just changes its label - you
won't get duplicates. Your primary home is its own slot, so the same house
can also be saved as a named or account home.

You can also save homes from the bars at the bottom of the window without
being in the house (see "Friend bar" and "Owned bar" below).


THE HOMES WINDOW (/homes)
-------------------------

The list has two sections: MY HOMES (this character's primary and named
homes) and ACCOUNT HOMES (shared by all your characters).

Each row shows, from left to right:
  - Teleport icon   A house icon for your primary home, a wayshrine for the
                    rest. Left-click to teleport.
  - @owner          Only shown for friends' houses.
  - Your label      The name you gave it.
  - Inside/Outside  Where a left-click or /home (/gohome) takes you for this
                    home.
  - House name      The game's name for the house. Hover it to see the zone,
                    click it to open the map at that house.
  - Edit (pencil)   Change the label, the house, or Inside/Outside.
  - X               Remove it from your list. (This only removes it from
                    Character Homes - it doesn't affect the house itself.)

Teleporting from the list
  - Left-click the icon to go to that home's default (Inside or Outside).
  - Right-click the icon on one of your own homes to pick Teleport Inside or
    Teleport Outside for just this trip.
  - Friends' houses always go inside. The game only lets you teleport outside
    houses you own.

Editing a home
  Click the pencil. The label becomes a text box, and for your own homes you
  also get a house dropdown and an Inside/Outside dropdown.
  - Save with the check mark or Enter.
  - Cancel with the X or Escape. Nothing is changed or deleted.
  For friends' houses you can only change the label.

Search and Owner filter
  - Search (top of the window) matches your labels, house names, zones and
    @owner names as you type.
  - Owner (top right) filters the ACCOUNT HOMES section: All account homes,
    My homes, or Friends' homes. It remembers your choice.

Friend bar (above the bottom bar)
  Friend: pick a friend, or a guild master or officer from your guilds.
  Type:   Primary, Named or Account wide - where to save it.
  House:  the house of theirs you want.
  Name:   an optional label.
  Then click the check mark to save it to your list, or the teleport button
  to go there right now without saving.

Owned bar (bottom)
  Lists the houses you own. Pick a zone to narrow the list, then choose
  Type, House and an optional Name.
  - Check mark: saves it to your list. With no name, it uses the house's name
    (or "Primary Residence" for a primary home).
  - Teleport button: goes there now. Right-click it to choose Inside or
    Outside.

Close the window with Escape, your Close Dialog key, or the X at the top right.


SLASH COMMANDS
--------------

/home or /gohome? /sethome or /setmyhome?
  Each pair does exactly the same thing - use whichever works for you:
    /home     = /gohome      (teleport)
    /sethome  = /setmyhome   (save the house you're in for this character)

  - /home and /sethome are the natural ones, and they're yours if you don't
    use Essential Housing Tools (EHT): not installed, or turned off in the
    add-ons list.
  - If EHT is turned on, it has its own /home and /sethome commands, so
    Character Homes doesn't take them over. Use /gohome and /setmyhome
    instead.
  - /gohome and /setmyhome always work, with or without EHT.

  Character Homes checks once you're logged in: if nothing else has claimed
  /home or /sethome by then, it uses it. So if one of them does something
  different for you, another add-on has it - just use /gohome or
  /setmyhome.

Teleporting (/home and /gohome are interchangeable in all of these)

  /home                      Teleport to this character's primary home,
                             inside or outside as set for it
  /home <name>               Teleport to the saved home whose label or house
                             name contains <name>, inside or outside as set
                             for it. If none match, it looks through the
                             names of houses you own.
  /home inside <name>        Same, but inside this time, whatever the setting
  /home outside <name>       Same, but outside this time (your own houses
                             only)
                             (Leave out <name> to use your primary home.)

Saving (/sethome and /setmyhome are interchangeable in these)

  /sethome                   Make the house you're in this character's
                             primary home
  /sethome <label>           Save the house you're in as a named home for
                             this character
  /setglobalhome <label>     Save the house you're in as an account home

Everything else

  /homes                     Open the Character Homes window
  /resethome                 Clear this character's primary home
  /delhome <name>            Remove an account home (part of the name is
                             enough)
  /delmyhome <name>          Remove one of this character's named homes (part
                             of the name is enough)
  /delmyhome primary         Remove this character's primary home

If more than one home matches, the addon lists them in chat so you can be
more specific.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

  - /home and /sethome: see "/home or /gohome? /sethome or /setmyhome?"
    above.
  - If another addon already uses one of the other commands, Character Homes
    leaves it alone and tells you in chat after you log in.
  - Bought a new house? Type /reloadui so it shows up in the Owned bar and
    the house dropdowns.
  - Your lists on the NA and EU servers are kept separately.
  - If Port to Friend's House is installed, a link to open it appears at the
    top of the window.
