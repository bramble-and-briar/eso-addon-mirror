Lazy Crown
Author: absolventR3M

Too lazy to lead? Let the crown decide. In Cyrodiil, Lazy Crown randomly
picks where to go next: a keep, lumbermill, farm, mine, town or outpost
your alliance doesn't hold, or one of your keeps or outposts that is
under attack. Who's wearing the crown decides how it picks and how it
talks, and every call goes on the scoreboard.

  "The crown doth declare an attack on Fort Ash. Ash till you crash."
      (Greedy)
  "I guess we should defend Nikel Outpost. We're gonna protect Nikel's
  pickle from tickling." (Ultra Lazy)
  "Alright, we are going to troll Kingscrest by sieging from the
  lumbermill." (November)
  "Let's be annoying and take Dragonclaw from the lumbermill tower."
      (Deano)
  "Wow losers... You're going to attack Bruma and you're gonna like it.
  Bruma is the key to the whole map guys." (Mean)

SIX WHEEL ENTRIES
  Crown Command (gold crown): your call-out. It also goes in the Chat
  page's text box, set to the channel you last used.
  Change Personality: puts the next personality on the crown.
  Scoreboard: places this run's record in the Chat page's text box.
  Reset Scoreboard: clears the record.
  Group Check: your group size, tanks, healers and DPS, and how many
  have Barrier, a necro rez or Negate slotted (chat window only).
  Private Command (crown gems): just for you, no call-outs. Where to go
  shows in your chat window only.

PERSONALITIES
  Who's wearing the crown changes how it picks. Choose one in Main Menu >
  Add-Ons > Lazy Crown > Personalities, or with Change Personality.
    Lazy        A lazy crown isn't always a lousy one.
    Bored       Chases fights and action.
    Chris       Lazy but with a brain.
    Confessor   Lazy as in not taking us all to our certain deaths.
    Deano       Scrappy small scale with a hint of troll.
    Diil        The real Diil, but I'm too lazy to siege for you.
    Greedy      Give me all that AP. All we do is win, win, win.
    Mulliss     Sometimes you just want to be a bad crown.
    November    Unconventional and favors flanks.
    Selfish     Looking out for number one.
    Ultra Lazy  Laziest crown ever.

TO SHARE IT: press X to send (Square on PlayStation). Press A to edit
(Cross on PlayStation). Nothing is ever sent unless you press Send.

FIRST CALL-OUT EACH SESSION: open Text Chat yourself once (Main Menu >
Text Chat) before using Crown Command. If an add-on is the first thing
to open Text Chat, the game blocks its Send button until you Reload UI.
Lazy Crown will refuse to open it and remind you.

THE SCOREBOARD
  Every call is checked when you make your next call.
  Taking: W if it's yours by then, otherwise L.
  Defending: W if it's still yours and the attack is over, L if it fell.
  Picking again within 90 seconds, or while a defense is still going,
  doesn't count against the last call, unless it was a defense that
  already fell. A call you're nowhere near by your next call doesn't
  count either, W or L. Following the hammer doesn't count.
  The scoreboard keeps this run's record until you reset it, even if
  you change personalities. All your characters share it.

WAYPOINTS
  Off unless you turn on "Set a waypoint" in Main Menu > Add-Ons >
  Lazy Crown > Options.

GROUP SIZE AND THE HAMMER
  Selfish, Chris, Greedy, Confessor and Mulliss size up the group: no
  outposts with fewer than 4 of you, no keeps with fewer than 6.
  When Volendrung is out, most crowns follow it if your alliance has
  it, and Greedy chases it no matter who has it.

TARGET TYPES
  The Keeps, Resources, Towns and Outposts switches in Options (all on
  by default) take those targets out of whichever crown you're using.

HOW THE NUMBERS WORK
Every target has a number from 1 to 81, and each number moves to the
next alliance's side of the map: 1 is an Aldmeri Dominion keep, 2 an
Ebonheart Pact keep, 3 a Daggerfall Covenant keep, 4 is the first
keep's lumbermill, and so on. Keeps and their lumbermills, farms and
mines are 1 to 72, the three towns 73 to 75, and the six outposts 76
to 81. /lazycrown list shows them all.

REQUIRES
  LibRadialMenu (version 9 or newer)
    https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info4297-LibRadialMenu.html
  LibHarvensAddonSettings (console only; LibRadialMenu needs it)
  Optional: LibGroupCombatStats (with LibGroupBroadcast), so Group
  Check can count everyone's ultimates, not just yours.

  On console, install them from the in-game add-on browser.

SETUP
  0. Open the Add-Ons menu and make sure Lazy Crown, LibRadialMenu and
     LibHarvensAddonSettings are all enabled, then Reload UI.
  1. Open the LibRadialMenu settings and set "Wheel Location" above 0
     (0 means the wheel is off).
  2. Under "Assign Slots", pick a slot and choose "Private Command" from
     the Lazy Crown section. Pick more slots for "Crown Command",
     "Scoreboard", "Reset Scoreboard", "Change Personality" and
     "Group Check" if you want them.
  3. Open your quickslot wheel, switch to the "Addon Entries" wheel,
     and pick.

  These instructions are also in game under Main Menu > Add-Ons >
  Lazy Crown.

  Where typing in chat is available:
    /lazycrown                   pick a target (chat window only)
    /lazycrown share             Crown Command: also place it in the
                                 Text Chat box
    /lazycrown scoreboard        show this run's record
    /lazycrown scoreboard share  place it in the Text Chat box
    /lazycrown scoreboard reset  reset it
    /lazycrown personality       put the next personality on the crown
    /lazycrown personality chris    (or any name) pick one
    /lazycrown group             group size, roles and big ultimates
    /lazycrown list              show every numbered target
    /lazycrown scan              print what the game reports (testing)

  Everything except Change Personality and Group Check only works in
  Cyrodiil.
