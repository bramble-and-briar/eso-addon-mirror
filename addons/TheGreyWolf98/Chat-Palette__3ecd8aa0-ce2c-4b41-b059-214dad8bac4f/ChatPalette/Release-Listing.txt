OVERVIEW
Your chat colours, every character. Set channel colours with hex codes or presets and share them across your toons on the same server. No dependencies.

DESCRIPTION
Chat Palette keeps your chat colours consistent across your ESO characters, including newly created toons. Choose a channel, enter a six-digit hex code or apply a preset, and your saved colour reapplies when you log into another character on the same server.

Customise Say, Yell, Zone, Group, incoming and outgoing whispers, Guild 1-5, Officers 1-5, Emotes and System messages. The controller-friendly menu displays the current hex value and an example message, with shared colours clearly marked.

Only channels you change become shared overrides. Reset any channel individually to ESO's default. No external addon libraries required.

Open /chatcolours, /chatcolors or /palette. D-pad selects a channel; A opens hex entry; LB/RB chooses a preset; X applies it; Y resets the selected channel; LT/RT changes page; B closes.

Hex codes accept six digits with or without #, such as #FF3333. Colours affect your own chat display. Guild and officer colours follow guild slots, not guild names. Saved palettes are separate for each server. Check new messages after changing a colour, as existing messages may retain their previous appearance.
