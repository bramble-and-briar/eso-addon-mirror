# Changelog

## 26.11.2

- Ultimate bars are now 2 pixels high and sit beneath group and raid frames.
- Improved spacing between Ultimate, damage and health values in small groups.
- Added German, French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese and Japanese translations.
- Settings are now saved separately for each server, preserving existing preferences.

## 26.11.1

- Restored shared DPS, HPS and Ultimate support with the updated LibGroupCombatStats library.
- OneFrame now shares these statistics directly through the library; Hodor Reflexes is no longer required.
