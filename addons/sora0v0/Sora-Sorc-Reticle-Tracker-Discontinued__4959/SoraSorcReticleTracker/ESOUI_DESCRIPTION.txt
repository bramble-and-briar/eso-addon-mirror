SORA SORC RETICLE TRACKER

A compact combat HUD for Sorcerer players who want to keep their eyes near the
reticle instead of repeatedly checking the action bar.

FEATURES

Crystal Fragments
- Displays the Crystal Fragments proc timer near the reticle.
- Works even when Crystal Fragments is slotted on the inactive weapon bar.
- Cyan Armory-style icon with the remaining proc time in the center.
- Independent size and position controls.

Bound Armaments
- Displays the current Bound Armaments stack count.
- Purple Armory-style sword icon.
- Stack colors:
  1-2: white
  3: gold
  4+: red
- Independent size and position controls.

OPTIONAL MAELSTROM BACK-BAR TIMER

A simple text-only timer is available for common Maelstrom Arena back-bar
ground skills. It only tracks a pair when the matching Maelstrom weapon is
equipped on the backup weapon bar and the matching skill is slotted there.

Supported combinations:
- Merciless Charge / Perfected Merciless Charge + Stampede
- Crushing Wall / Perfected Crushing Wall + Elemental Blockade or Unstable Wall of Elements
- Thunderous Volley / Perfected Thunderous Volley + Endless Hail

Timer colors:
- More than 5 seconds: white
- 5 seconds or less: gold
- 2 seconds or less: red

Each Maelstrom timer type can be enabled or disabled individually.

DEPENDENCY

LibAddonMenu-2.0 r43 or newer.

SLASH COMMANDS

/ssrt crystal
/ssrt bound
/ssrt maelstrom
/ssrt stop
/ssrt reset
