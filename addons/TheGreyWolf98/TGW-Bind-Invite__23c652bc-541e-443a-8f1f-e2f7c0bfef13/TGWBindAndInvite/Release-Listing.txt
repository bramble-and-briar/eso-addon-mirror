TGW Bind & Invite
Version 1.0.0
By @TheGreyWolf98

Keep control of your loot and get your group together with one controller-friendly addon.

Manual binding lets you finish a dungeon or trial, offer gear to your group, then
bind the uncollected pieces you keep. Review items individually in the preview,
or use /tgw bind to bind one eligible copy of each missing backpack collection
piece. Spare duplicates are left alone, the lowest-quality eligible copy is
preferred, and locked items are protected. Optional AutoBind handles newly
received uncollected pieces when you want to collect as you go.

AutoInvite sends a group invitation when someone whispers your chosen exact
keyword. Choose a controller preset or set your own with /tgw keyword yourword.
Dungeon and trial limits, leadership checks, duplicate protection and pending
invite reservations help keep recruitment under control.

Both automatic features are independently toggleable and OFF by default.
Includes a controller menu, binding preview, instructions and session log.
Account settings are saved separately per server. No external libraries required.

Commands
/tgw or /tgwbi — open the menu.
/tgw bind — immediately bind one eligible copy per missing collection piece.
/tgw keyword yourword — set your custom invitation keyword.

Binding removes trading rights. Preview exclusions last for the current session;
use the game's item lock for lasting protection. Invites use ESO whispers, not
Xbox private messages. Disable equivalent AutoBind/AutoInvite features in other
addons, including NQOL, to avoid overlapping automation.
