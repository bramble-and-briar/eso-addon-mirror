OVERVIEW
Find your next skill points with automatic character progress tracking for dungeon
quests, zone quests, skyshards, public-dungeon group events and Alliance Rank.
Browse saved characters in a controller-friendly window. Open with /spf.

DESCRIPTION
Skill Point Finder helps you see where each of your characters still has skill
points to earn, without manually ticking off their existing progress.

Track base-game and DLC dungeon skill-point quests, zone quest rewards and
skyshards, Cyrodiil, Imperial City, Alliance Rank and public-dungeon group events.
The Other category includes The Harbourage, level awards and the Infinite Archive
introduction.

Log into each character once to capture their existing history automatically,
then use the character selector to review any saved character. Offline characters
show their last saved scan. Supported progress changes refresh automatically.

The zone view groups EP, DC and AD in story progression order, with DLC zones in
release order across two columns. Separate quest and skyshard colours make missing
progress easy to spot. Dungeon entries use clear completion checkboxes.

Open with /spf. Use LB/RB for categories, the D-pad for entries and columns,
A for details, X for characters and Y to scan your currently logged-in character.
No external libraries required.

Dungeon completion refers to the one-time skill-point quest; public-dungeon
completion refers to the group event. Available points and skyshard counts are
shown separately. Unsupported optional or legacy rewards are marked unknown.
Catalogue coverage and limitations are documented in README.txt.
