ATLAS MINI MAP
By @TheGreyWolf98

A north-up minimap with clock, FPS, latency, quest and service icons, waypoint direction and antiquity dig areas. Optional Atlas layers add your enabled map pins.

Now includes 4,357 recorded fishing spots across 106 map tiles, with different fish icons and colours for ocean, lake, river and foul water. Fishing works independently of Atlas and MasterBaiter. Pins mark possible spawn sites, not currently active fishing holes.

Open /minimap to adjust size, zoom, position and display switches. Toggle Fishing spots independently. X opens Socials / Support; B returns or closes. Existing settings carry over. No external libraries required.
