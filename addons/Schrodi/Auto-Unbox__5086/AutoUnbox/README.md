# Auto Unbox

Opens every container as soon as it lands in your backpack. A much smaller take on Unboxer: no categories, everything that is a container gets opened.

- **Automatic opening** of new containers (can be turned off in settings).
- **Don't open automatically:** unidentified survey reports, unknown writs and unopened treasure maps (each one separately). These only affect automatic opening.
- **Unbox All** button in the inventory opens every container in the backpack, including the skipped ones and even with automatic opening turned off. While it runs, the button cancels it.

Opening waits while you're in combat, swimming, dead, looting, talking to an NPC, at a station, bank, vendor or mailbox, or in the housing editor, and stops when the backpack is full.

Not touched: runeboxes and style pages (they're collectibles, not containers), and transmutation geodes while you're at the crystal cap. A container that fails to open three times in a row (e.g. it holds a unique item you already have) is skipped with a chat message.
