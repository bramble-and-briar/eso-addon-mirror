## Title: BTV Tools Roster Importer
## Author: BallTongue
## Version: 0.3.0
## AddOnVersion: 300
## APIVersion: 101048
## Description: Paste a roster export from BTVTools.com to get a full Wizard's Wardrobe setup ready to go. Automatically match items from your bags to the setup and get a list of items needed to be crafted, fetched, reconstructed or farmed, do them by hand or enable auto-craft to let the addon create the items needed for you.
## DependsOn: WizardsWardrobe>=1232 IIfA LibAddonMenu-2.0 LibSets
## OptionalDependsOn: LibLazyCrafting
## SavedVariables: BTVToolsVars

## Wizard's Wardrobe is where the setups live, IIfA is how "you own this, it is just on
## another character" gets answered, LibAddonMenu draws the settings panel (#463), and
## LibSets knows whether a set is crafted and where it drops, for the Missing gear step
## (#479). All four are required, none is optional. LibLazyCrafting is optional: only the
## plan's auto-craft queue needs it (#482); without it the craft rows say so.

## BTVToolsVars holds the settings and the import record (#463, #467): which import is in
## progress, on which page, plus a rolling history of done ones.

## Version is the release, AddOnVersion is that same number encoded (0.1.0 -> 10, 1.2.3 ->
## 10203). Both move together on every release. The payload version is a third number that
## lives in the code, not here, and moves only when the payload format itself moves (#395).

## The WW floor is the version its internals were read against. DependsOn cannot express an
## upper bound, so a newer WW that moved them loads us happily; BTV.Import checks the paths
## it reaches for by hand and refuses by name instead of failing somewhere deep (#395).
