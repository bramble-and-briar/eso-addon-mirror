ATLAS 1.0.3
by @TheGreyWolf98

OVERVIEW
Your map. Your layers. Atlas brings skyshards, lorebooks, treasure-map digs,
crafting surveys, bosses and discovery locations together in one compact,
controller-friendly panel, with account-wide settings and optional filtering
for the maps and surveys in your backpack.

DESCRIPTION
Explore Tamriel with the map information you want, all controlled from one
screen. Atlas combines thirteen individually switchable map layers:

- Skyshards and recorded Shalidor lorebooks
- Treasure-map dig sites and crafting surveys
- Recorded delve and public-dungeon interior bosses
- Delve and public-dungeon entrances
- World bosses and dolmens/world events
- Wayshrines and striking locales
- Mundus stones and set crafting stations

Open /atlas and use the D-pad to select a feature, A to toggle, Y to refresh
and B to close. PlayStation uses ESO's corresponding button prompts.
All seventeen feature/filter/visibility switches fit on one panel; settings are shared
across your characters. PC users can also click the feature rows.

Hide collected removes acquired skyshards and known lorebooks for the current
character. Carried maps / surveys only shows recorded sites for maps and
reports in that character's backpack, helping you focus on what you can use.
The relevant map or report is still required to dig or harvest.

IMPORTANT: Remove or disable other add-ons that provide the same skyshard,
lorebook, treasure-map, survey or boss pins before using Atlas, to avoid
duplicate markers and competing pin layers. If keeping an overlapping addon
for features Atlas does not cover, disable its matching pin layers or Atlas's
matching layer. A map display such as Votan's minimap can remain enabled;
Atlas's pins use ESO's normal map-pin system.

Coverage varies by zone and map floor. Group-dungeon interior boss coverage
is incomplete; Atlas does not promise every boss in every dungeon. Lorebooks
are recorded Shalidor locations, not a complete Eidetic Memory database.
Event markers show locations, not live spawn alerts. Wayshrine markers do
not unlock travel. Side-quest start markers are not included.

No external addon libraries are required.

NEW IN 1.0.2
Neon colours: bright coloured rings help pins stand out while retaining
their original symbols. Toggle off for the classic palette.
Larger pins: choose standard (28) or 50% larger (42) Atlas symbols.
Both options apply immediately from /atlas and save across characters.

1.0.3 LIVE DISCOVERY FIX
Location discovery, POI updates, objective completion and wayshrine unlocks
now trigger a debounced pin refresh. No movement or UI reload required.
Existing settings, neon colours and larger-pin switch are preserved.
