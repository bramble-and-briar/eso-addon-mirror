Bind and Invite
Author: @TheGreyWolf98
Version: 1.0.2

Collect missing set pieces and manage keyword whisper invites from one controller menu.

• Preview backpack gear, skip pieces and confirm before binding.
• Optional automatic binding for newly received uncollected set pieces.
• Optional whisper invites with a custom keyword and 4- or 12-player limit.
• Larger Atlas-style menu, session log and socials page with Ko-fi QR.

/bi — menu
/bind — binding preview
/invite — invite settings
/invite @name — normal group invitation
/bi keyword yourword — custom whisper keyword

Both automatic features start OFF. Binding ends item trading. Xbox messages do not trigger invites; use ESO whispers.
