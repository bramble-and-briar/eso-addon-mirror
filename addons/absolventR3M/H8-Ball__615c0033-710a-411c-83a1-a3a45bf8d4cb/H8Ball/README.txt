H8 Ball
Author: absolventR3M

Shake the H8 Ball. You know you want to. Your answer shows up in your
chat window and is pasted into the Chat page's text box, set to the
channel you last used. Press Send to share it, or back out to keep it
to yourself. Shaking from the wheel opens the Chat page for you.

REQUIRES
  LibRadialMenu (version 9 or newer)
    https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info4297-LibRadialMenu.html
  LibHarvensAddonSettings (console only; LibRadialMenu needs it)

  On console, install them from the in-game add-on browser.

SETUP
  1. Install H8 Ball, LibRadialMenu and, on console,
     LibHarvensAddonSettings.
  2. Open the LibRadialMenu settings and set "Wheel Location" above 0
     (0 means the wheel is turned off).
  3. Under "Assign Slots", pick a slot and choose "Shake the H8 Ball"
     from the H8 Ball section.
  4. Open your quickslot wheel, switch to the "Addon Entries" wheel,
     and select Shake the H8 Ball.

  Where typing in chat is available, you can also type /h8ball.
