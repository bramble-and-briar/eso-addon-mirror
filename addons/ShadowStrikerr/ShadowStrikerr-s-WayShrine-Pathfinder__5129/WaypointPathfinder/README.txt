ShadowStrikerr's WayShrine Pathfinder 0.1.0
In-game name: WayShrine Pathfinder

Extract the WaypointPathfinder folder into Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns.
Restart ESO after installing the textures and enable WayShrine Pathfinder in Add-Ons.
For updates, replace the add-on folder and keep your SavedVariables.
New settings and recent walks may be lost if ESO closes before saving.

Place an ordinary personal waypoint on the world map and return to the gameplay HUD.
The distance panel shows horizontal straight-line distance. Arrows use a locally
recorded route when one connects with your position. A direct bearing is unverified
and can cross terrain or obstacles. Arrival is within three horizontal meters.
The waypoint stays in place until you clear it. A waypoint is followed only when
ESO exposes valid coordinates on the supported overland map matching your location.

Click the cyan marker to collapse and pause. Click the compact icon to reopen
and resume or drag it to reposition it. Drag the title or grip to move or resize
the expanded panel. Closing with × hides the panel while guidance continues.
To clear a personal waypoint through ESO, hover over its map diamond and use Set Destination.
Click Auto Wayshrines to clear the selection and show trails to nearby
discovered wayshrines. It defaults to three trails and can show one to five.
The panel lists each displayed wayshrine and its straight-line distance, nearest first.
When Follow recorded walked paths is enabled, each automatic trail uses a connected
recording when available. It uses an unverified bearing when no route can continue.
Select a personal waypoint at any time to show just that trail.
The active waypoint has a gold outline on the world map while guidance is on.
Wayshrine tooltips show Click F to set target or Following when selected.
Use ESO's Set Destination key if it has been rebound.

Chat commands: /wpf help, panel, move, trail, pause, resume, settings, status.
The trail command toggles ground arrows. The panel command reopens and resumes.
If /wpf is already registered, use /wayshrinepathfinder or /shadowstrikerrwayshrinepathfinder.
Assign shortcuts in ESO Controls under WayShrine Pathfinder.
Target hovered wayshrine starts unbound. Assign a key, hover over a wayshrine
on the keyboard world map, and press it to place a personal waypoint.

No dependencies are required. LibAddonMenu-2.0 adds the AddOn settings page with
seven ground styles, including Wayshrine Arrow, plus distance colors and visibility options. AUI - Advanced UI is
optional. The optional trail dots appear only on AUI's minimap and default to off.
