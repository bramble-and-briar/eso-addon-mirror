Miracle's BetterScoreboard
Enhanced PvP scoreboard featuring 18-player support, damage dealt, healing, damage received, and K/D statistics. Includes player names, class icons, battleground queue features, and Deathmatch lives positioning adjustments.

Original addon creator: @M0R_Gaming (BetterScoreboard).
Fork updates and direction: @MlRACLE.
This is a separately named community-maintained fork, not an official update by the original author.

Compatibility: Do not enable the original BetterScoreboard or ScoreboardFixU51 alongside this fork; they modify the same ESO scoreboard.
Saved settings: Character-ID scoped saved variables under MiraclesBetterScoreboardVars. Separate addon settings are not imported from the original BetterScoreboard.
