## Title: Execute Blocker
## Description: Prevents casting of execute abilities when the target's health is above a configurable threshold. Uses LibExecuteSkills for the skill database. Supports Nightblade, Templar, Sorcerer, weapon skills (2H / Dual Wield / Bow), and Warden Guardians. Includes optional soft fade flash feedback when a cast is blocked.
